Realme Note 60x Overview

The Realme Note 60x is one cheap smartphone that gives the user an appropriate performance as adequately as the basic necessities. It runs on Unisoc Tiger T612 chipset technology. This device comes equipped with a 6.74 inch display. For the photography lovers, it comes equipped with a quad camera setup on the back panel which includes a 8 MP primary camera, and an 0.03 MP of Auxiliary lens camera. The front-facing camera has 5MP sensors.

The phone has a 5000mAh battery, long-lasting usage is guaranteed and it’s charged at the rate of 10-watts fast charging. It works on Realme UI 5.0 based on Android 14. The Realme Note 60x price in Bangladesh is BDT 10,999 (Official) for 4GB of RAM and 64GB of phone storage.
